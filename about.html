THE IDEA

Technology that turns awareness into action.

Waste sorting can be confusing. A simple bottle, container, or piece of packaging may not always be easy to classify correctly.

EcoScan was created to make that decision easier. Users can capture or upload a waste image and receive an AI-generated recognition result together with practical information.

Instead of stopping at identification, EcoScan connects the result to responsible waste-management actions and environmental data.
